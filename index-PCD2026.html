Info

Processing Community Day (PCD) is a worldwide series of community-led events celebrating art, code, and open-source creative tools. Events are organized by local communities around the globe.
→ Processing Community Day

Processing was created in 2001 as a creative tool for artists and designers. Today, we celebrate 25 years of contributions and look forward to everything still to come.
